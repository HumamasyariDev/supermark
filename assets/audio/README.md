# Super Mak 64 audio bank (night 01, M3)

Owner: audio workstream. Two entry points:

1. **Juice events (preferred).** Gameplay code calls `Juice.event(name, position, params)` (Docs/JUICE.md). Juice installs
   `juice_sfx.gd` as its child `JuiceSfx`, which plays the sound layers in `juice_sfx.json` (135 events; 8 dead duplicate rows were removed in fix stage r1b).
2. **Direct keys.** `Audio.play_sfx/play_voice/play_music/play_jingle/play_ambience(key)` with the keys in
   `audio_index.json` (498 keys, 415 files). Unknown keys only warn. Every key the player, enemies, boss,
   objects, worlds and UI scripts call today resolves, including wrapper calls (`_sfx(`, `EnemyKit.sfx(`,
   `moment_sfx(`, `call("play_sfx", ...)`): `_src/tools/key_audit.py` scans `game/scripts` and fails on a missing key,
   on a looping key called through a one-shot path, or on an attack whoosh that peaks outside its strike window.

```gdscript
Juice.event("player_land", global_position, {"impact": 11.0, "surface": "wood"})
Juice.event("enemy_defeat", head_pos, {"family": "gummy_brute", "node": self})
Audio.play_ambience("amb_w1")            # area beds: amb_hub, amb_w1, amb_w2, amb_w3, amb_boss
Audio.play_jingle("truffle_get")         # resolves to jingle_truffle_get on the Jingle bus
```

## juice_sfx.json (generated by `_src/tools/juice_sfx_build.py`; edit that file)

Per event: `layers` (several sounds at once), random variants (lists in the index), `pitch_jitter`, `volume_jitter_db`,
`delay` / `delay_jitter`, `chance`, `cases` (switch layers by params: `kind`, `impact`, `heavy`, `surface`, `family`,
`step`, `hat`, `liquid`), key templates (`footstep_{surface}` with `fallback`), per-event `cooldown` (scoped per `family`
for enemies) and `max_per_second`, per-layer `cooldown`, `pitch_by` (page index, ring index), `chain` (acorns rise one
semitone per pickup within 0.7 s, up to 10), `volume_by` (speed, impact, bounce velocity), named loops (`loop_id`,
`max_time` safety stop, `stop`), voice layers (`voice: true`, `priority`, per-line cooldown, global 0.25 s gap; a
higher priority line interrupts, an equal one waits), `positional` and 3D `attenuation` presets:

| Preset | unit_size | max_distance | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| near | 8 m | 45 m | pickups, foley, cloud steps |
| default | 6 m | 60 m | enemies, objects |
| far | 12 m | 110 m | impacts, boss, door unlocks |
| huge | 20 m | 160 m | sleeping giant, tornado |

Player events are 2D (Mak is always mid-frame); UI events are 2D and skipped when `ui_fx.gd` already played them
(`params.ui_sfx`). `silent: true` in params skips the sound.

**Ownership (no double sounds, no gaps).** Each emitter asks before playing its own sound:
- **Player** (`mak_player.gd _fb()`): plays nothing itself whenever `Juice.has_event()` is true, so JuiceSfx owns every
  player event (no `handoff`). Until the implementation stage the player events still carried `handoff: "owns"`, so in
  real play Mak's sounds came from `juice_vfx.json`'s one-key fallback (always `footstep_grass`, no voices, no layers).
- **Enemies** (`EnemyKit.juice_sfx_owns` per moment): JuiceSfx plays the per-family table; the enemy passes `silent`
  for moments it voices itself.
- **Objects** (`ObjSvc.feedback` / `juice_handles`): JuiceSfx plays; the object's fallback key is skipped.
- **Boss**: `wolf_boss.gd` emits 15 `boss_*` events and skips its own sound wherever `Juice.handles(event, "sfx")`
  is true. Since the fix stage (critique r1 blocker) the boss rows have no `handoff`, so JuiceSfx plays them; the table
  leaves out what the wolf still plays itself (its voice lines, the charge growl, the tail line).
Labs and recordings can pass `--juice-sfx-all` to hear the boss events, and `--juice-sfx-log` prints every play
(`[JuiceSfx] <t> <event> -> <key> <dB> x<pitch>`), which is how real-play recordings are checked.

**Ground surface by world.** The player reports `grass` for any floor without a `surface` meta or group.
`surface_world_default` in the JSON swaps that default per world (HUB wood, W3 candy, BOSS stone); W1 and W2 keep it.
Floors with their own meta are unaffected.

**Area beds (fix stage r1).** `ambience.worlds` lists each world's layers. JuiceSfx plays them on its own players (not
the Audio autoload's single ambience player, whose stale stop tween killed the new bed after transitions): 3-4 loops
with co-prime lengths (layer A 20.5 s, layer B 13.7-17.3 s, the old single loops 5.9-8 s) at random start points, plus a
scatter emitter (one-shots every few seconds 6-70 m around the camera on 4 private 3D players: hub crackle, page turns
and a distant chime; W1 bird phrases; W2 high chimes; W3 chocolate plops; boss thunder in phase 3). The bed starts in the
frame a new world appears (equal-power fade-in), holds through the scene swap and checkpoint reloads, fades under the
page curl when the Router heads to another world (`Router.transition_started`; the music fades there too) and fades out
when no world is left. An explicit `ambience_key` is left to the Audio autoload; old single loops in `upgrade` (the
boss's `amb_storm_loop`) are stopped. The boss layers carry per-phase levels: phase 1 dry sunset wind, phase 2 light
rain, phase 3 the full storm, phase 4 calm wind after the defeat (`boss_phase` and `boss_defeat` drive it).
**Space.** `ambience.reverb` puts a reverb on the SFX and Voice buses per world (hub room 0.55 / wet 0.18, small open
slapback elsewhere, off on the title). **Polled states** (`states`): the wall-slide loop follows the fall speed, the
slide loop's pitch and volume follow the slide speed (and it also starts on slopes), climb steps play while Mak moves on
a pole, and a soft pulse plays at one slice of cake. **Proximity loops** (`proximity`): truffle sparkle near live
truffles, gusts near wind zones. **Pause menu:** Music and Ambience go through a 900 Hz low-pass at -6 dB, and SFX that
were playing pause. **Voice:** a line still playing that JuiceSfx did not start (wolf, dialogue) counts as priority 4,
so hero grunts never cut it. 3D plays beyond their preset's max distance are skipped, a key never starts twice within
60 ms (`allow_stack` overrides), and the shared Audio pool slot gets its own attenuation back when the sound ends.
**Moments:** a lethal hit skips the hurt layers (`skip_if_lethal`), the death ducks the music 14 dB for 1.4 s, the
respawn sting is skipped for 1.5 s after a scene change (world entry), and the boss defeat fades the battle music,
calms the bed and starts `music_hub_lively` once the victory jingles are over.
Objects already defer to Juice (`ObjSvc.juice_handles`), so object events play now. Labs and recordings can pass
`--juice-sfx-all` to hear everything.

**Mix helpers at runtime** (`mix` in the JSON, applied once by JuiceSfx): a compressor on the Music bus side-chained
from Voice (threshold -28 dB, ratio 3, release 350 ms) so dialogue sits on top, and a hard limiter on Master
(ceiling -0.5 dB) so stacked impacts never clip, plus a limiter on SFX (ceiling -2 dB).

## Mix (measured, `_src/tools/make_index.py`)

Every one-shot and voice file is level-matched to L100 = -16 LUFS (`level_match.py`: the loudest 100 ms, K-weighted;
ebur128's 400 ms momentary window under-reads sub-0.4 s sounds), so variants of one key sit within about 1.5 dB.
Files that would need gain but have no peak headroom are skipped: before this rule every rerun pushed the same 13
transient-heavy files through the limiter again (found in the implementation stage, one extra pass happened).
Each key's `volume_db` then places it at its group target (`group` and `att` fields in the index):

| Group | Target | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| music (integrated) | -24 LUFS (hub +2, boss -2.5 dB: real play measured 6.7 LU apart) | 7 loops |
| jingle (integrated) | -19 LUFS | victory, truffle get, menu sting (Jingle bus) |
| ambience (integrated) | -30 LUFS (was -31; critique round 1) | area beds and old loops |
| voice (L100) | -16 | Mak, Huffington, Bramwell |
| impact | -15 (was -14; critique r1: SFX bus peaked above 0 dBFS) | ham_slam, land_heavy, crate_break, chimney, boss crash |
| reward | -15 | truffle_get, truffle_appear, trial results, door unlock |
| boss / boss_voice | -15 | boss inhale, charge, growl, hurt |
| pickup | -17 | acorn, page, heal, checkpoint |
| world_big | -17 | giant snore, sneeze, inhale |
| enemy | -18 | notice, hurt, per-family vocalisations |
| action | -19 | jumps, landings, attacks, splashes, bounces |
| world | -19 | bell, chime, gates, doors |
| ui | -21 / -25 soft / -28 text blip | |
| foley | -24 | ledge grab, pole grab, skid, sniff |
| footstep | -25.5 (was -27; critique round 1: steps read as clicks under the music) | 8 surfaces |
| loops (integrated) | -29 / -30 bee | slides, gusts, tornado, sparkle |

In-engine check (Movie Maker, `tests/AudioMixPreview.tscn`): World 1 with music, the meadow bed and scripted gameplay
sounds measures -20.8 LUFS integrated with -0.5 dBFS peak; music plus ambience alone sits at about -25 LUFS and gameplay
sounds 4 to 5 LU above it. The truffle jingle now plays 7.8 LU above the ducked music (`tests/JingleCheck.tscn`).

## Loops

`_src/tools/seam_check.py` decodes every looping file: the wrap jump must be smaller than the file's own 99th-percentile
sample step (no click) and the 50 ms level change across the wrap no larger than the file's own 95th percentile at
random points. 41/42 pass after r1b (r1b loops are rotated so the wrap sits at a quiet, steady point); earlier: 35/36 pass (`_src/reports/seam_check.json`); `amb_w2_la` has a wrap step of 1.02x its own 99th
percentile step (the PCM wrap is continuous; the step comes from Vorbis encoding, not a click). Godot plays plain loops (`loop_offset = 0`; see below).

## Godot finding: avoid `loop_offset`

An intro-plus-loop version (Ogg `loop_offset` = loop start) was tried and rejected in round 1: decoding through Godot
4.7's `AudioStreamPlayback.mix_audio()` (`tests/loop_wrap_dump.gd`) showed the first wrap at `length - loop_offset`.
Plain loops wrap exactly at the end of the file.
